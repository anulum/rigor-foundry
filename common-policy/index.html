    Rule declarations and original components¶

    The provisional RuleScope1, SemanticDecision1 and AtomicRule1 records represent exact source-bound declarations. They retain the original ConditionExpression 1.1, SourceSpan1, OwnerProof1 and TypedObjectRef1 contracts. They do not authenticate an issuer, accept semantic extraction or admit a policy for execution.

    Every parser and serializer requires an explicit PolicyReadBudget1. Document bytes and container nesting are checked before child parsing; collection owners also enforce source and aggregate reference limits. Inputs with unknown fields, unsupported versions, reordered sets, duplicate identities, invalid UTF-8 or incorrect digests refuse. For file input, use read_policy_document from rigor_foundry.policy_source_reads before the record's from_dict method. That reader also rejects duplicate JSON keys and uses bounded stable file reads.

    Typed v3 signatures¶

    TrustRolesV3 in rigor_foundry.rule_chain_trust_v3 binds five exact original OfflineTrustPolicy objects. Original lifecycle validation enforces distinct key identifiers and public keys across roles, v1 nonsemantic keys, and v2 semantic keys with disjoint enrolled author/reviewer identities. Its trust_roles_digest is the canonical digest of schema_version="3.0" and the five named original policy digests: pack_issuers, project_selectors, lock_resolvers, semantic_authors, and semantic_reviewers. Any change to any selected policy changes this generation identity, including an unrelated role's rotation or revocation metadata.

    rule_chain_v3_signature_boundary resolves the closed kind/body-version/role/ domain table. RuleChainV3Signature, in rigor_foundry.signed_rule_chain_v3, parses exactly twelve fields and requires an explicit read budget. Its payload binds signature schema, kind, body schema, domain, canonical body digest and UTC signing time; its signature digest hashes actual Ed25519 bytes.

    The factory normalizes accepted UTC timestamp spellings with the original timestamp owner before storing them or deriving the signed payload. Serialized records use that canonical spelling ending in Z. Direct records and wire objects carrying a noncanonical alias refuse instead of emitting a record that cannot roundtrip. Normalizing a factory input preserves its actual payload and signature bytes.

    project-profile 3.0 project-selector rigor-foundry.project-profile-selection.v3

    verify requires the consuming owner's independently expected kind, body version/digest, selected whole-policy digest, current UTC instant and complete policies. It reparses metadata and every original policy, then verifies the actual signature and key activity both at signing and now. Future timestamps, expired/revoked/unknown keys, changed role policies and cross-version replay refuse. Both corpus roles sign the same acceptance body under distinct domains.

    This is a signature primitive over an already validated body digest. The consuming body owner must separately parse its complete actual pack, profile, lock or corpus-acceptance body and establish its trusted source. This API does not itself parse or admit those bodies, ratify semantics, enroll keys, select a host or grant effects. Original v2 signatures retain their original grammar and domains; the new provisional modules do not change the stable1.0 surface.

    Scope and exact statements¶

    A global scope has no condition and explicitly lists every supported task event. A scoped rule has an original condition and a nonempty sorted event subset. Missing facts remain unknown under evaluate_condition_state; structural scope parsing does not authenticate fact producers or decide a gate.

    from rigor_foundry.policy_read_budget import PolicyReadBudget1
from rigor_foundry.rule_scope import RuleScope1
from rigor_foundry.task_context import TASK_CONTEXT_EVENTS

limits = PolicyReadBudget1.build(
    max_document_bytes=65536,
    max_source_bytes=65536,
    max_total_source_bytes=131072,
    max_sources=16,
    max_rules=64,
    max_references=1024,
    max_depth=32,
)
scope = RuleScope1.build(
    scope_kind="global",
    condition=None,
    events=tuple(sorted(TASK_CONTEXT_EVENTS)),
    budget=limits,
)
assert RuleScope1.from_dict(scope.to_dict(budget=limits), budget=limits) == scope

    AtomicRule1 hashes the statement's exact UTF-8 bytes, including whitespace and original newline characters. Source spans retain their declared selection order. Dependencies and decision IDs are sorted unique sets; a rule cannot depend on itself. Component references sort by (kind, format_version, object_id) and cannot give two digests to one identity. Proposed, active, held, superseded and retired declarations remain explicit. An active lifecycle string is not semantic acceptance.

    SemanticDecision1 retains the issuer, original spans, rationale, condition, affected rules, successors and original owner proof. Its relations are supersedes, exception, exclusion, interpretation and adoption. Supersession has nonempty distinct directed rule sets. Complete graph resolution, competent issuer authentication and exception permission remain required at the consuming boundary; dates, filenames and matching hashes establish none of these.

    Explicit review and evidence contracts¶

    rigor_foundry.review_policy provides provisional ReviewRoleRequirement1 and ReviewPolicy1 records at version 1.0. Each role binds an exact original reviewer identity policy and an explicit independence requirement. A review policy retains its mode (independent, self-review or conditional-self-review), sorted unique roles, source rule IDs and named unavailability fact references. Parsing these declarations does not appoint reviewers or establish that a manager is unavailable. Empty declarations grant no fallback or approval. A consuming assessment must verify actual role eligibility, source permission and authenticated unavailability before allowing conditional SelfReview.

    rigor_foundry.evidence_contract_v2 provides EvidenceRequirement1 at 1.0 and EvidenceContract2 at 2.0. Each requirement retains an evidence type, exact adapter reference, subject class and exact original freshness-policy reference. A contract requires a nonempty sorted unique requirement set and an explicit review-policy reference. Aggregate reference limits apply before child parsing. The contract does not invent numeric freshness or reviewer counts; legacy EvidenceContract records keep their original positive TTL, adapter and evidence sets, and minimum independent reviewer count.

    The new review and evidence records expose build, to_dict and from_dict with an explicit read budget. ReviewPolicy1.reference and EvidenceContract2.reference bind their complete owning digests. Resolve their targets through the corresponding new owning parser; the original context-source resolver does not acquire these formats merely because a typed reference parses.

    Rule-bound qualitative controls¶

    rigor_foundry.control_definition_v2.ControlDefinition2 is provisional at 2.0. It binds an explicit rule ID, original scope, require/deny mode, qualitative evidence contract and unchanged original remediation contract. Domain, severity and target maturity retain their existing vocabularies. It has no implicit default applicability or separate legacy condition field.

    control.verify_rule_binding(rule, budget=limits) accepts an actual AtomicRule1 and rechecks both complete records. Rule ID, mode and complete scope must agree, and the rule must contain the exact control reference, including its owning digest. The new boundary also closes the original remediation's seven fields while preserving its exact text, order and obligations. Original ControlDefinition 1.0 serialization remains unchanged.

    Atomic enforcement references allow original control 1.0, authority 2.0 and freshness 2.0, together with qualitative control 2.0 and review-policy 1.0. Bare adapters and reviewer identity policies are support dependencies; they cannot become atomic enforcement components. Structural control binding does not establish source acceptance, applicability, evidence validity or a gate verdict.

    Original signed-pack bindings¶

    rigor_foundry.component_source_resolution.parse_policy_component_target resolves fourteen closed policy target formats: the eight original component formats listed above; new ControlDefinition2, EvidenceContract2, ReviewPolicy1, EvidenceReference2 and SemanticDecision1; and original StandardPackV2 at 2.0. Each fixed branch compares the complete actual target reference, including its original ID and owning digest. Unsupported pairs refuse before target parsing; the context-source resolver keeps its separate supported subset.

    StandardPackSource2 bounds the complete original pack before its existing recursive serializer/parser runs. It requires actual original child types and immutable collections for direct instances, and exact arrays for wire input. Control counts and aggregate references include every control, condition, authority grantor and freshness invalidator. All original controls, child wires and detached signature bytes remain present. Its reference uses original pack_id and complete pack_digest; the original pack version remains inside that digest. Reading a pack does not verify current trust or semantic acceptance.

    rigor_foundry.legacy_control_binding.LegacyControlBinding1 is provisional at 1.0. It binds a source rule to an exact original control 1.0 within an original signed pack 2.0, together with sorted unique source decision IDs. binding.verify_pack(pack, budget=limits) revalidates the complete actual pack and returns its exact matching original control. Empty packs, absent controls, different owning digests and qualitative-control casts refuse. Original target maturity, applicability condition/default, numeric freshness, reviewer count, adapter/evidence requirements and remediation remain intact. This membership check does not establish complete selected-control enumeration or permission for an exception, waiver or gate approval.

    Qualitative freshness declarations¶

    rigor_foundry.freshness_proof provides provisional InvalidatorObservation1 and FreshnessProof1 at 1.0. An invalidator retains clear, invalid or unknown explicitly. clear requires an original OwnerProof1; a parsed proof envelope still needs authentication by its installed current owner.

    A freshness proof binds the exact original freshness-policy reference, mode, independent subject digest, original subject proof, sorted unique invalidator observations and subject snapshot. Its subject proof must match that digest and snapshot. Invalidator owners retain their own source snapshots. No duration, expiry or observation timestamp is inferred. Explicit legacy expiry and key or grant lifecycle rules remain separate.

    proof.verify_policy_binding(policy, budget=limits) requires an actual original FreshnessPolicyV2 and compares its complete identity, mode and invalidator set. This checks structural binding. Invalid and unknown observations remain present; the method does not authenticate clear or return a freshness verdict. For every invalidator, the installed verifier must resolve its actual selected owner, source contract and independent current observation, then establish that the ID/state governs this outer subject and its relevant state or generation. Missing, unrelated, contradictory, stale, revoked or unauthenticated observations cannot establish clear; invalid and unknown cannot pass. Held source checks remain required through use and closure.

    The invalidator record's observation_digest includes its proof envelope. The original owner's observation_digest hashes its independently observed ContextProducerObservation1 preimage. These digests are distinct; equating them would introduce a circular proof. freshness_reference_count(value, budget=limits) lets enclosing consumers bound the complete nested allocation before parsing children. It counts the policy, subject-proof pair, invalidator IDs and optional original proof pairs.

    Independent evidence subjects¶

It hashes the exact adapter reference, artifact digest and byte count, configuration, toolchain, cohort and complete sorted unique source-object references. No producer proof, freshness proof or enclosing evidence digest enters this subject.

    Provisional EvidenceReference2 at 2.0 adds the evidence identity/type, classification, opaque reference, validated UTC observation time, declared result, original producer proof and qualitative freshness proof. Both included proof subjects must match the independently computed evidence subject. Producer and freshness owners may retain different source snapshots. Changed artifact, source, configuration, toolchain or cohort identities cannot retain the old proof binding. All nested reference allocations and source counts respect the explicit budget.

    Results pass, fail, unavailable and refused remain declarations until the installed adapter parses the actual retained artifact and current owners verify its complete scope and proofs. Neither result text nor structural parsing grants approval. The opaque reference field embeds no raw artifact or credentials and does not grant permission to read a path or URL. object_reference(budget=limits) returns its exact typed evidence identity, separately from that artifact reference.

    evidence.verify_requirement_binding(requirement, policy, budget=limits) checks the actual EvidenceRequirement1, adapter, evidence type, subject class and original freshness policy. It supplies no assessment verdict. The original numeric EvidenceReference keeps its exact separate wire and expiry semantics; neither parser migrates the other format.

    Shared original assessment predicates¶

    rigor_foundry.assessment_evidence_validation factors the existing legacy predicates into three functions. They consume original validated contract, adapter, evidence, attestation and trust-store inputs; they create no profile or lock and grant no source selection or operational authority.

    • validate_original_evidence_bindings requires one exact original adapter identity and owning digest for each reference, covering the control domain.
    • validate_original_pass_evidence retains the original positive numeric age bound, explicit expiry, nonempty evidence and complete required adapter/type coverage. Every submitted item must be fresh; a fresh member cannot hide another expired or future observation.
    • validate_original_reviewers retains actual Ed25519 verification of the exact original assessment body and decision, current review interval, explicit trust store and required distinct reviewer IDs, key IDs and public keys. The original case-sensitive identity comparison remains unchanged.

    The existing public ControlAssessment now calls these same predicates. Its wire 1.1, original review-body identity, status/applicability, waiver and limitations rules remain unchanged. New selected consumers must supply their own actual selected source/control relations and independently derived review body; these functions do not appoint owners or infer SelfReview permission.

    Complete quality projection¶

    rigor_foundry.quality_projection_v3.QualityProjectionRowV3 retains an exact control reference, source rule IDs, scope, evidence contract, reviewer policy, target maturity and applicability decision references. Its format is 3.0.

    The legacy1 case binds the actual original control and numeric evidence contract at 1.0. It preserves the original Boolean default_applicable, nullable original condition and target maturity; review_policy is null because the original positive reviewer count remains in the evidence contract. The qualitative2 case binds the actual qualitative control and evidence contract at 2.0, with its explicit review-policy reference. Its default and condition are null because its RuleScope1 owns applicability.

    row.verify_control_binding(control, budget=limits) reparses the actual complete owning control and compares every projected obligation. Qualitative rows also retain the control's exact scope and source rule relation. Rehashing a row cannot repair a different control digest, evidence contract, review policy, maturity or original applicability field.

    validate_quality_projection(rows, controls, budget=limits) requires exactly one complete row per supplied selected control, sorted by its exact versioned identity. It validates both actual control generations and the complete cohort, including aggregate reference counts and encoded array bytes. Missing, extra, duplicate or reordered rows refuse. The enclosing owner must derive the supplied controls from its actual selected signed generation; this structural check does not establish selection or evaluate applicability.

    Applicability decisions use exact SemanticDecision1 references at 1.0. decision.reference(budget=limits) binds the complete declaration's original ID and digest; the fixed policy target resolver reparses its actual owner. Issuer authentication, source acceptance and permission for exceptions remain separate required checks. None of these APIs supplies a gate verdict.

    Selected assessment inputs¶

    rigor_foundry.selected_control_assessment_v2.SelectedControlAssessment2 uses the exact 2.0 wire. It binds the selected lock, quality projection, task and event request digests; the actual control generation; explicit applicability and status; evidence and review references; assessor, timestamp and reason. legacy1 references original evidence at reference format 1.0; qualitative2 references EvidenceReference2. Both retain the actual original signed review attestation format 2.0. Reference identities are sorted and unique under complete byte and allocation bounds.

    Applicability is the string true, false or unknown, separately from any legacy Boolean default. Unknown cannot pass, and false remains explicitly unassessed. A declared status, including pass or accepted-risk, supplies no authentication or gate result. Current contract, evidence, reviewer and exact source exception verification remain the installed consumer's responsibility; accepted risk cannot replace a mandatory source floor's pass.

    assessment.verify_quality_binding(row, control, budget=limits) verifies the actual complete quality row and owning control generation. The enclosing owner must independently derive the complete selected cohort and verify the lock, projection, task and request identities; coordinated subsets of the row/control inputs cannot prove that completeness.

    assessment.review_body_digest(budget=limits) hashes every assessment field except reviews and the final assessment_digest. Reviews can therefore sign the independent complete input before their references are added. Changing a lock, projection, task or request changes that body; adding the resulting review references changes the final assessment digest without changing its review body. Original Ed25519 attestations keep their original signature domain, exact body binding and validity checks. A digest or parsed signature alone authenticates neither a reviewer nor a selected generation.

    validate_selected_legacy_pass consumes the exact selected assessment, quality row and original control alongside complete original adapter, evidence and attestation cohorts, an explicit original public trust store and an aware evaluation instant. It revalidates all complete owners, matches the declared evidence/review references and applies the same original numeric freshness, adapter/type coverage and positive independent reviewer predicates used by ControlAssessment. Actual original Ed25519 signatures bind its independently derived review body. Missing trust, self review, insufficient distinct quorum, stale/future/expired evidence and changed request bodies refuse. No v1 lock is created. Complete selected-root/source custody, artifact/result observation, key lifecycle and closing verification still belong to the installed consumer.

    Supporting evidence contracts, evidence references, reviewer identity policies, attestations, adapter custody and semantic decisions cannot become atomic enforcement components merely because their fixed target readers exist.

    Complete declared rule graphs¶

    rigor_foundry.source_semantic_graph.source_rule_dependency_order revalidates the complete sorted rule and semantic-decision cohorts. It resolves every declared dependency, decision and affected/successor target, including held and historical rows. A rule's decision reference must actually name that rule in the decision's affected or successor set. Endpoints are checked even when the rule has no redundant backlink; an empty source-only decision creates no rule.

    The complete declared dependency relation and the complete declared supersedes relation must both be acyclic. Conditional decisions and inactive lifecycle labels cannot hide a declared supersession cycle. Interpretation, adoption and exclusion metadata do not become supersession edges. Directed set expansion has an allocation bound before any Cartesian edge set is materialized.

    The returned IDs contain every supplied rule exactly once, after its dependencies. Simultaneously available nodes sort by exact ID; this ordering does not select semantic precedence. Complete encoded metadata bytes, source counts and declared reference allocations are bounded across both cohorts.

    The installed owner must still authenticate the actual complete source cohort, issuer competence, scope and current condition facts; independently derive all effective decisions; check simultaneous conflicts and prove exception authority. Caller-selected omissions or a successful graph traversal establish none of those requirements and grant no gate result.

    The retained source universe separately verifies the complete host-selected original source cohort and byte partitions. Neither that byte accounting nor these declaration records establish semantic completeness. Source access, issuer decisions, corpus acceptance, current host selection and permission for effects remain separate owning checks.

    Deterministic source document inputs¶

    rigor_foundry.rule_source_document.RuleSourceDocument1 retains the complete source universe, original byte regions, atomic rules, semantic decisions, qualitative controls, review policies, legacy control bindings, original freshness policies and authority references. Each collection has a fixed child owner; extra fields, duplicate identities, mutable direct collections and changed child digests refuse. Original children keep their complete original wire.

    Collections sort by their exact identities. Regions retain (source_id, start_byte) order. Legacy bindings use the composite (rule_id, pack.object_id, control.object_id): a rule may bind multiple original controls and an original control may have several source rules. Repeated triples refuse even when their binding digests differ. Digest ordering never selects a different convention.

    Explicit aggregate reference, byte, collection and nesting limits cover the whole document, including its envelope and final digest. Original region links, source counts and freshness invalidators are bounded before their original child parsers allocate the corresponding records. The separate compiler_contract_digest identifies the compiler contract input. Serialization adds no timestamp, resulting pack/profile/lock, signature or corpus acceptance. The document digest hashes every other complete listed field deterministically.

    from_dict checks bounded structural integrity; it does not resolve external typed targets, accept source extraction or authenticate semantic decisions. The consuming compiler must still verify the complete original source cohort, same retained bytes and region/span joins, all component and decision relations, original pack membership and actual source/issuer/corpus acceptance. A structurally valid document supplies none of those permissions by itself.

    Source compilation with retained owners¶

    rigor_foundry.rule_source_compiler.compile_rule_source_document takes the existing SourceUniverseOwner and complete explicit rule, region, decision and component cohorts. It obtains the universe from that actual owner. There is no caller-supplied replacement universe, filesystem discovery or prose interpreter. Repeated compilation of the same complete inputs and held owner state is identical; the compiler adds no timestamp or resulting selection.

    rule_source_compiler_contract_digest() separately identifies the document schema, fixed target table, composite legacy order, complete declared graph, component joins and retained source checks. This contract identity is separate from the document digest, actual implementation custody and semantic acceptance.

    verify_rule_source_document revalidates the complete document and returns all rule IDs in dependency-first order. It verifies the actual complete source cohort initially, resolves every supplied external component through its fixed owning parser, and rejects inconsistent versioned target identities even in unused support objects. Whole target bytes and typed-reference counts are bounded together with the document. Original pack members and their nested numeric evidence contracts keep their actual original identities.

    Every rule component must be declared by the complete emitted cohort, and every emitted component has an owning rule. Active rules require enforcement components and a declared normative source region. Qualitative controls retain exact rule/scope/mode bindings; their evidence references resolve the actual inline review/freshness policies, exact adapter and matching subject class. Review policy source-rule IDs equal the rules that actually reference it. Required reviewer identity policies resolve as complete original objects.

    Legacy bindings verify the exact original pack member. All controls, authority clauses and freshness policies of each referenced original pack remain accounted for; a valid pack signature cannot excuse a dropped control. Signature bytes, old numeric evidence and original conditions are preserved. Parsing these records does not authenticate current signing trust or transition scope.

    Source/region/rule/decision links resolve in both directions. Every citation has an overlapping declared same-source region, and every region's declared rule/decision has a matching citation. Half-open byte intervals do not overlap at adjacent endpoints. Lookup uses indexed source relations and sorted interval indexes without rewriting the retained citation order. Duplicate span IDs must refer to identical complete spans. Rule output classification cannot be less restricted than its cited source.

    The same actual source owner finally verifies complete byte partitions and original span bytes, including closing source and implementation checks. Held or excluded sources cannot supply included citations. A source-only exclusion can retain an explicit exclusion decision without inventing a rule endpoint; that declaration still needs actual competent issuer and corpus acceptance.

    The result remains a compiled source candidate. Declared byte overlap does not prove adequacy of the extracted prose. Competent issuer/effective decision verification, independent corpus/native ratification, current root selection, evidence authentication, gate evaluation and effect authority remain separate required owning boundaries.

    Complete corpus acceptance¶

Its nine fields include both headers, corpus ID, document, universe, decision, component and compiler digests, and the native ratification reference. Universe and decision digests hash their complete wire arrays. The component digest hashes the complete five-key mapping containing qualitative_controls, review_policies, legacy_control_bindings, authority_bindings and freshness_policies, including empty arrays.

    RuleCorpusAcceptance1 adds the full author and reviewer signatures and its final digest, giving exactly twelve fields at schema 1.0. Both signatures bind the same unsigned body under their separate v3 domains. Parsing validates their complete metadata, kind, body version and body identity. Current crypto, key lifecycle and protected policy selection remain consuming checks, so an old envelope remains structurally readable without becoming currently valid.

    rigor_foundry.rule_corpus_ratification.RuleCorpusRatification1 retains exactly eleven fields at 1.0: both headers, corpus/document/universe/decision identities, issuer ID, original native-pin reference, original statement span, statement digest and final ratification digest. Its reference and span must name the same original source. The record contains neither a host provider nor an issuer-enrollment instruction.

    Exact native corpus statement¶

    render_rule_corpus_question, in rigor_foundry.rule_corpus_native, renders the entire actual document as JSON with sorted keys, compact separators, unescaped Unicode and no nonfinite values. Fixed surrounding prose requests this exact decoded user text:

    SCHVAĽUJEM rule-corpus <corpus_id> <document_digest> <universe_digest> <decision_set_digest>

    The rendered question and expected user text have no terminal LF. Original native capture processing appends exactly one LF to each decoded message. A user-authored terminal LF therefore produces two capture LFs and refuses the exact statement grammar. No stripping, whitespace normalization, NFC conversion or abbreviated document is accepted. The complete rendering, including the capture owner's LF, must fit the explicit document budget.

    RuleCorpusNativeOwner borrows actual RetainedContextSource pin metadata, the entire RetainedPolicySource JSONL and an already-held original HostNativeSourceState. Installed composition separately supplies the competent issuer, exact corpus scope and protected expected issuer-binding digest. rule_corpus_native_binding_digest hashes the fixed rigor-foundry.rule-corpus-native-issuer-binding.v1 contract identifier, those two IDs and the complete original native reference. Computing this digest does not authenticate its selection; it must match independently installed state.

    Verification joins the complete question, exact decoded statement, original selected user role/message/line identity, later reply timestamp, full file hash/path, captures, current selected pin and revocations. The statement span covers the entire original selected user JSONL line, including carrier LF; bounded scanning derives its exact offsets from that same retained file. Three SHA-256 preimages stay distinct: decoded statement UTF-8; the same text plus one capture LF; and the whole original JSONL line. Escaped Unicode in the carrier does not change the decoded statement or permit a plaintext span. Closing rechecks the whole borrowed snapshot without closing its owning leases.

    Held corpus verification¶

    rigor_foundry.rule_corpus_verification.RuleCorpusVerifier.verified_context composes the actual source/compiler, native and selected-signature owners. It compares the complete accepted unsigned body with the actual document and ratification, verifies both real signatures against the whole selected policy generation and current installed clock, and runs the complete original source compiler and target checks.
